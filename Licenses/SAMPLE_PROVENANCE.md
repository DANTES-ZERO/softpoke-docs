# Sample content: provenance and AI disclosure

SoftPoke 1.0.0. This file describes where the content in `Content/SoftPokeSample/` (the sample character, its hands and hair, the baked data, and the two demo maps) comes from. Everything outside `Content/` is original SoftPoke code and documentation (see `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`).

**Character created with Charakuru (by Nano).**

The sample character is an adult woman. Nothing in the package, its documentation or its promotional material presents her in a sexual way.

## What is in the sample

| Part | Where it is | Origin |
|---|---|---|
| Body, head and clothes (mesh `SK_FEM3D_F`, subdivided display mesh `SK_FEM3D_Fsub1`, textures in `Character/Tex`) | `Content/SoftPokeSample/Character` | An original character design. Reference images were generated by the publisher with AI image-generation tools. The 3D mesh was generated from those images with Tripo (the publisher's paid plan) inside Charakuru, a character-creation tool by Nano. The publisher then re-authored the skeleton (Unreal Mannequin / MetaHuman-compatible bone names) and the skin weights, corrected the mesh and fixed seams and other defects in the textures. |
| Hands (`HandNatural/SKM_NatHand2_R`, `SKM_NatHand2_L`) and the hand look materials (`HandLook`) | `Content/SoftPokeSample/Character` | The hand geometry is from MakeHuman (CC0 1.0, public domain), re-weighted by the publisher on a hand-only skeleton. The materials are original. |
| Hair (`Groom`) | `Content/SoftPokeSample/Character/Groom` | Strand hair made by the publisher with his own Blender strand-hair add-on and imported as Unreal Groom assets (strands only; no hair cards are included). The simulation settings and the scalp tint textures are original. |
| Baked SoftPoke data (`SPB_*`, `SPF_*`, `SPK_*`, `SPKP_*`) | `Content/SoftPokeSample/Character` | Generated by SoftPoke's own bake tool from the character mesh above. |
| Deformer graphs and dent materials | `Content/SoftPokeSample/Deformer` | Original (built with the engine's Deformer Graph; they call the engine's stock deformer functions). |
| Demo maps and demo pawn / game mode | `Content/SoftPokeSample/Maps`, `Demo` | Original. They use the engine's own sky sphere. |

* The weights of the Charakuru donor body may be transferred to a new mesh. Transplanting the donor mesh or its bones, or extracting the donor body on its own, is not allowed. The sample character was checked for this: its mesh, bones, skin weights, UVs and textures contain no data of the donor body.
* No credit wording is required and no limit on how buyers may use the sample was requested. As a courtesy, this package carries the line above.

Evidence of the paid Tripo plan: the Tripo **Professional Plan** (billing period 2026-10-11 to 2026-11-11, invoice issued 2026-10-11). The character mesh was generated on this plan (the character export is dated 2026-10-12). The publisher keeps the invoice, the written exchange with Nano and the Tripo terms on file; they are not part of this package.

## AI-generated content disclosure

* The sample character (reference images, 3D mesh and textures) was made with the help of generative AI (image generation, then image-to-3D with Tripo). The character is original and does not depict a real person or an existing character.
* The SoftPoke plugin itself was developed with AI assistance (code and documentation).
* The Fab listing for a package that contains this sample must carry the **Created with AI** tag (`CreatedWithAI`). The publisher confirms the tag on the Fab submission form.
